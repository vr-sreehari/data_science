Manufacture:
New -> 60% -> 98%-Good 2%-Defect
Old -> 40% -> 95%-Good 5%-Defect

Defect product from old machine probability?
A->Production from old machine
B->Produced defect product
P(A|B) = P(B|A) P(A) / P(B)
P(A) = 0.40
P(B) = 0.05
P(B|A) = 0.02
P(A|B) = 0.02 * 0.40 / 0.05
       = 0.008/0.05
       = 0.16 -> 16% chance
